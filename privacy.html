Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Summary

M-Cleaner Pro does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

What the app accesses

After you grant permission through a standard macOS panel, the app reads file names and sizes inside the folders you allow (your home folder and, for the Uninstaller, the Applications folder) to show what uses disk space. This happens only on your Mac. File names and contents are never sent anywhere.

What the app changes

The app only moves files to the Trash or deletes them when you select them and confirm. It never changes anything on its own.

Data stored on your Mac

The app keeps a small local history of past cleanups (date, size, number of items), your folder permissions and the amount of free cleaning used. This stays on your Mac, and you can clear the history in the app.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details or personal information.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
