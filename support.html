Common questions

The app shows "macOS doesn't let M-Cleaner Pro read this folder"

macOS protects some folders (for example device backups). Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and check the permissions for the app, or skip that item.

I'm asked to choose my home folder

This is a one-time macOS permission for sandboxed apps. Choose your home folder (the one with your user name) and click Grant Access. Do the same for Applications when you open the Uninstaller.

An app could not be moved to the Trash

Some apps need administrator rights. Drag them to the Trash in Finder instead.

How do I restore my subscription?

Click "Unlock Pro" in the sidebar, then "Restore Purchases". You must be signed in with the Apple Account that subscribed.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open System Settings, click your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel M-Cleaner Pro. You can also use apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
