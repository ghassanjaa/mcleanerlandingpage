FAQ

Why does it ask for access to my home folder?

Apps from the Mac App Store run in a sandbox. You grant access once through a standard macOS panel, and the app can only reach what you allow.

Can I undo a cleanup?

Yes. Items are moved to the Trash, never deleted permanently by the app, so you can put them back until you empty the Trash. Review the list before you confirm.

Do you collect any data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.

I subscribed on another Mac. Can I restore?

Yes. Use "Restore Purchases" in the unlock screen with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel?

Open System Settings, click your name, then Media & Purchases or Subscriptions, and cancel M-Cleaner Pro. You keep Pro until the period ends.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon and Intel.
